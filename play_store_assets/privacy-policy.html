Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Beta is a cart-only grocery assistant. It helps you add requested items to a supported grocery app cart. It stops before checkout and payment so you can review the cart yourself.

Information we use

  • Your item requests and order instructions.
  • Screen text, screen capture, and accessibility data from supported grocery apps only after you start a flow.
  • Basic device and app info such as app version, build type, device model, Android version, and order result.
  • Optional feedback and optional diagnostic logs if you choose to send them.

How we use it

  • To understand what you want added to the cart.
  • To find products, tap supported grocery app controls, and verify the cart.
  • To improve reliability and fix problems reported through feedback or logs.
  • To run the Beta service through our backend on Google Cloud.

What Beta does not do

  • It does not start screen capture or accessibility access until you begin an order flow.
  • It does not complete checkout or payment.
  • It does not place orders on your behalf.

Your choices

  • You control when a flow starts and can stop it at any time.
  • Feedback and logs are optional.
  • You can clear saved preferences in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your data by emailing gandharv@musicaigeneration.com.

Sharing and storage

We send data to Beta's backend on Google Cloud so the app can work. We store optional feedback separately from order-related telemetry.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: gandharv@musicaigeneration.com
