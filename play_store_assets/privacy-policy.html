Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Beta is a cart-only grocery assistant. It helps you add requested items to a supported grocery app cart, currently Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, or Zepto. It stops before checkout and payment so you can review the cart yourself.

Information we use

  • Your item requests and order instructions.
  • For Swiggy Instamart: your saved-address choices, product search results, go-to items, order history and order details from the last 15 days, and current cart contents, accessed through your secure Swiggy connection only when needed for a flow you start.
  • For Blinkit and Zepto: screen text, screen capture, and AccessibilityService data only after you start a cart-building flow.
  • Visible Blinkit or Zepto screen details that may include product names, prices, cart contents, buttons, and delivery details such as your name, precise delivery location, delivery address, locality, or delivery-area header text if those details are shown by the grocery app.
  • A random installation identifier used to protect your Swiggy connection. It is encrypted on your phone; the backend stores only an opaque derived identifier.
  • Basic device and app info such as app version, build type, device model, Android version, and order result.
  • Optional feedback and optional diagnostic logs if you choose to send them.

How we use it

  • To understand what you want added to the cart.
  • To learn what you usually mean by an item, recommend matching products, show exact cart changes, add only after your confirmation, and verify the resulting cart.
  • For Blinkit and Zepto, to tap visible grocery app controls and confirm that the cart-building flow is using the delivery context visible on screen.
  • To improve reliability and fix problems reported through feedback or logs.
  • To run the Beta service through our backend on Google Cloud.

What Beta does not do

  • The Swiggy connection does not use screen capture or AccessibilityService.
  • For Blinkit and Zepto, it does not start screen capture or AccessibilityService access until you begin an order flow.
  • It does not complete checkout or payment.
  • It does not place orders on your behalf.
  • It does not sell personal or sensitive user data or use grocery screen data for advertising.

Your choices

  • You control when a flow starts and can stop it at any time.
  • Feedback and logs are optional.
  • You can disconnect Swiggy in the app, which removes Beta's stored Swiggy connection, and reconnect later.
  • You can clear saved preferences in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your data by emailing gandharv@musicaigeneration.com.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of Beta data, email gandharv@musicaigeneration.com with the subject line Delete my Beta data. Please include any tester email address you used and a short note that the request is for the Beta app. We will review and action deletion requests within 14 days.

Sharing and storage

We send data to Beta's backend on Google Cloud so the app can work. The Android app never stores your Swiggy access token or OTP. The backend stores the per-user Swiggy access token encrypted and removes that connection when you disconnect. We store optional feedback separately from order-related telemetry.
