Beta Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Beta is a cart-only grocery assistant. It helps you add requested items to a supported grocery app cart, currently Swiggy Instamart or Blinkit. For Swiggy Instamart, Beta uses MCP first and keeps a reversible screen-assisted fallback if needed. It stops before checkout and payment so you can review the cart yourself.

Information we use

  • Your item requests and order instructions.
  • For direct Swiggy Instamart use: a pseudonymous Beta installation identity, an encrypted Swiggy connection token, saved address details, address-specific product results and availability, go-to and recent-order product history, current cart contents, and the saved address you select.
  • For Blinkit and the optional Swiggy screen-assisted fallback: screen text, screen capture, and AccessibilityService data only after you start a cart-building flow.
  • Visible supported grocery app screen details that may include product names, prices, cart contents, buttons, and delivery details such as your name, precise delivery location, delivery address, locality, or delivery-area header text if those details are shown by the grocery app.
  • Basic device and app info such as app version, build type, device model, Android version, and order result.
  • Optional feedback and optional diagnostic logs if you choose to send them.
  • If you grant location permission, Beta reverse-geocodes your current location on your phone only to rank nearby saved Swiggy addresses. Raw GPS coordinates are not sent to Beta's backend or stored by Beta.

How we use it

  • To understand what you want added to the cart, including learned shorthand grocery preferences when you use them.
  • To find products currently available for your confirmed delivery address and rank them using your learned preferences and recent choices.
  • To add only the items you confirm and verify the resulting cart.
  • To improve reliability and fix problems reported through feedback or logs.
  • To run the Beta service through our backend on Google Cloud.

What Beta does not do

  • It does not start screen capture or AccessibilityService access until you begin a cart-building flow.
  • It does not complete checkout or payment.
  • It does not use GPS as the authoritative delivery selector or silently change your delivery address.
  • It does not see or store the phone-number OTP you enter on Swiggy's secure sign-in page.
  • It does not place orders on your behalf.
  • It does not sell personal or sensitive user data or use grocery screen data for advertising.

Your choices

  • You control when a flow starts and can stop it at any time.
  • Beta may suggest a saved Swiggy address using recent choices and, with optional permission, an on-device current-location match. You still choose and confirm the address and can change it before a cart update.
  • Feedback and logs are optional.
  • You can clear saved preferences in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your data by emailing gandharv@musicaigeneration.com.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of Beta data, email gandharv@musicaigeneration.com with the subject line Delete my Beta data. Please include any tester email address you used and a short note that the request is for the Beta app. We will review and action deletion requests within 14 days.

Sharing and storage

We send data to Beta's backend on Google Cloud so the app can work. Swiggy connection tokens are encrypted at rest. We store optional feedback separately from order-related telemetry.
